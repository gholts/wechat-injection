Build and sign an IPA locally. Your files stay in this browser.






Download signed IPA

Select profiles for WeChat and <WeChat App ID>.WeChatStickers, with the same App Group and signing certificate. Include profiles for existing extensions. Enter an App Group if the profiles share more than one.

After installation, open WeChat’s heart tab, then WeChat Stickers in Messages.

Use a desktop browser for large IPAs. Certificate revocation is not checked.
